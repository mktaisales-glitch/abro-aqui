# Spec 06 — UI

One page, no build step, mobile first. English with a Spanish toggle.

**Top:** concept (text), address (text), optional second address ("compare with"), button "Should I open here?".

**Loading:** four steps shown as they complete — "Locating the address", "Reading the area's taste", "Finding competitors", "Writing the verdict". Target < 25 s cold, < 5 s cached.

**Result, in this order:**

1. Verdict card: traffic-light badge (RED / AMBER / AMBER-GREEN / UNKNOWN), one-sentence headline, the rule that fired (small, expandable).
2. Heat map (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap), 1.5 km, cells coloured by affinity, the address pinned, the best alternative cell pinned.
3. Competitors table: name, rating, price level, distance.
4. The gap: categories the area likes but nobody offers.
5. Who passes by: aggregated taste profile (tags) and affine brands as ad interests.
6. Three actions: concept positioning, opening hours, up to three partners, only Qloo names confirmed on the web (the list is hidden on the page; the launch plan carries the partners).
7. What Qloo does not know: three questions (foot traffic, rent, income) with input fields; answering all three upgrades AMBER-GREEN to GREEN locally.
8. Chat panel: "what if…?" with suggested chips.
9. Tab "How I know": every call made, without the key, with timing and cached flag.
10. Toggle "Without Qloo": the same report generated by the LLM alone.

**Layout v3 (28-Oct-2026), after user testing: plainer, Google-like, mobile designed.**

- **Search card** at the top of the left panel (white, 16 px radius, soft shadow, 8 px grid): a segmented control (*Should I open my idea here?* / *I have premises: what fits?*), then the fields and one big orange button (#EF751F, hover #D4421B). Michroma only for the logo and the big headings; Montserrat for everything else; black text; orange for active states, icons, links and the verdict card's top bar.
- **Type of business** is a combobox (input + `role=listbox`, arrow keys, Enter, Escape; mouse and touch) over `GET /api/concepts`: the 15 concepts the demo knows how to read, with English and Spanish labels and the words people type for them (hamburguesería, cafetería, heladería, vermut…). A synonym selects its concept. Text that matches nothing shows "Not available yet — choose one from the list" / "Aún no disponible: elige uno de la lista" and nothing is sent. The server maps ids, labels and synonyms too (`src/concepts.js`).
- **Address** is an autocomplete: after 4 characters (350 ms debounce) `GET /api/geocode/suggest` lists up to 5 places (label + country, from Photon, whose policy allows autocomplete). A picked place sends its exact coordinates (`coords`) with the label. Typed without picking, the server geocodes it and prefers the city the address names; if it can only find the street elsewhere, the verdict card says "Is this the place?" / "¿Es este el sitio?" with the address it used and a button to fix it. Placeholders: "Avinguda del Paral·lel 4, Barcelona" / "Avenida del Paralelo 4, Barcelona".
- **Verdict card**: verdict word, headline, the concept and address it analysed (pin icon, bold), three facts, next steps (*Find a better spot*, *Plan the opening*, *Ask Open Here*) and "which rule decided it".
- **FAQ sections** (accordion, several can be open, the first one open; `button[aria-expanded]` + region): *Why this result?* · *Who goes out around here?* · *Is there a better spot nearby?* (scout run) · *How do I launch the opening?* (launch run) · *How do we know?* (every call / without Qloo / compare). Spanish: *¿Por qué este resultado?* · *¿Quién sale por la zona?* · *¿Hay un sitio mejor cerca?* · *¿Cómo lanzo la apertura?* · *¿Cómo lo sabemos?* Next-step buttons open their section and start the run.
- **Download PDF**: once there is a result, a pinned orange button with a download icon at the bottom of the results panel (a fixed bottom bar on phones) links to `/api/report.pdf?id=…&lang=…`. There is no separate report section: everything on the page is the report.
- **Assistant**: not in the left panel. *Ask Open Here* opens a chat floating over the map, bottom-right (a full-screen sheet on phones), closable with × or Escape, keeping its history for the current result. First open: a greeting naming the concept, address and verdict, and five chips: *Summarise the report in 3 sentences* · *What would make this Promising?* · *What are the main risks here?* · *And within 500 m?* · *Ignore the closest competitor* (and their Spanish twins). The model gets the whole report (verdict, facts, rule, competitors, area, report text) and answers short and plain in the page language, without a tool when the report already holds the answer; `**bold**` shows as bold. When the daily model budget is used up it says so: "Today's free limit for the assistant is used up on this demo" / "El límite gratuito de hoy del asistente en esta demo se ha agotado".
- **Phones (390 × 844)**: one column — search card, verdict, the map at 40 vh, the sections, footer. No sideways scroll, tap targets at least 44 px, the *Ask Open Here* button fixed bottom-right above the PDF bar (it steps aside while the card's own button is on screen), map legend collapsed at the top.
- Map style unchanged (grey OpenStreetMap tiles, five orange classes, green ring for the better spot). Every string has its Spanish twin.

**Brand:** titles Michroma, body Montserrat, accent #EF751F, light background. Footer: responsible-use note (aggregated data, no personal data, not for housing, employment or credit decisions).

**Errors:** address not found → suggest fixing; Qloo error → retry button; LLM down → template report with a notice.
